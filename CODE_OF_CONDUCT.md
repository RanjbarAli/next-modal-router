# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward other participants. Harassment, intimidation, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
